Hermes Publisher

Hermes Publisher is the publishing tool behind a small family of short-video channels: Pantesan Laku (why shops sell), Pantesan Bisa (how everyday things work), Same Income, Night Shift Choices and Kawa & Orbo. We make every video ourselves, and this tool sends the finished videos to our own TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. A channel owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and allows Hermes Publisher to upload videos to that account.
  2. When a video is finished, Hermes Publisher uploads it to the account with TikTok's Content Posting API, as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post.
  3. The owner opens TikTok, checks the video, adds the caption or sound and posts it. Nothing is posted to an account that has not signed in.

What we use from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the account's name and avatar, so we know which channel we are uploading to.
  • video.upload / video.publish: to upload our own videos to that account.

We do not read followers, messages, likes or other people's content.

Questions or a request to remove data: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository (link at the bottom of the repository page), or revoke access at any time in TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
