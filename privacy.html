Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains what Hermes Publisher ("we") collects when a TikTok account owner connects their account, and how it is used.

Who uses Hermes Publisher

Hermes Publisher is used only by the owners of our own channels to upload videos we produced to their own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the general public.

Information we collect

  • From TikTok, after you sign in: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues for the permissions you approved.
  • Videos and captions that we produced ourselves and upload to your account.

We do not collect your password, contacts, messages, followers, location or payment information.

How we use it

  • To upload our videos to the TikTok account you connected, as a draft or a post, using the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • To show which account a video was sent to and whether the upload succeeded.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Tokens and upload records are kept on our own private server, readable only by the account that runs Hermes Publisher. This website itself stores nothing: the sign-in page only shows the one-time code TikTok returns so the owner can finish the connection.

How long we keep it

Until you disconnect. When you revoke access we delete your tokens; upload records are kept at most 12 months.

Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time in TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • Ask us to delete your data by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository.

Children

Hermes Publisher is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly connect accounts of minors.

Changes

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes.
